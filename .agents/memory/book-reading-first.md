---
name: Book reading before recitation
description: Current text-first, Tarteel-inspired Nawawi recitation direction superseding the earlier scanned-page UI.
---

Use beautifully typeset interactive text, not scanned page images or image crops,
for the reading and recitation experience. Read first, and progressively reveal
matched words only after an explicit recitation start. Returning to reading must
stop microphone capture.

**Why:** On 2026-10-03 the user superseded the earlier request for original page
images: “لا أريدك أن تستخدم الصورة مباشرة”. They named Tarteel (not talqeen)
as the interaction reference and requested an attractive, fast word-reveal
experience rather than simply hiding words.

**How to apply:** Keep reading, selection and recitation as distinct modes.
Do not default entry to hidden words or leave the complete text visible during
active recitation. Keep source scans for reference verification, not as the
required learner-facing rendering layer. The user subsequently explicitly
authorized implementation of this direction.

The intended scope is the complete Forty Nawawi collection, from beginning to
end, including sanad, matn and the narrator/source attribution printed with each
hadith, not isolated matn-only sessions. Use numbered pages, manual page
navigation and automatic continuation to the next page when recitation reaches
the current page end, including hadiths spanning pages. Keep the surface focused,
not a dashboard. Matched words should appear smoothly and mismatches should be
visibly signalled; recognition uncertainty must remain explicit.

**Why:** On 2026-10-03 the user said “الأربعين النووي بالكامل ... من الألف إلى الياء”
and explicitly requested automatic page transitions and the attribution after
the hadith. This changes the practice scope, not verified grading rules.

**How to apply:** Never call matn-only practice complete-book recitation.
Preserve the selected edition's text and passage order across page boundaries.
Do not invent source attribution or silently append editorial commentary to
the recitable text. Improved rendering does not prove recognition accuracy;
experimental alerts remain separate from grades and permanent learner errors.

Keep digital practice pagination explicitly distinct from printed page numbers.
Use the existing full textual source consistently for the complete collection;
do not combine its sanad or attribution with the separate Dar al-Salam scan text.

**Why:** The scan-derived practice excerpts do not contain full sanad and
attribution for the collection. The user's move away from images permits a text
experience, not silently claiming two different sources are the same edition.

**How to apply:** Show the textual source and label digital page numbering.
Keep assessment passages unchanged. Author introductions and editorial margins
are not automatically included when the user asks for complete hadiths.

The experimental no-grading/no-transcript-storage boundary does not forbid
saving an explicitly chosen reading position.

**Why:** Replacing the study surface must not break returning to the last
selected hadith or the dashboard's continuation link.

**How to apply:** Preserve ordinary navigation progress and existing bookmarks;
never turn recognized words into assessed completion, grades or saved mistakes.
