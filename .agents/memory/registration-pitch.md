---
name: Registration pitch framing
description: Distinguish the Mateen concept-registration pitch from final-product claims and disclosures.
---
The hackathon registration presentation must describe the idea and proposed plan, not showcase implementation, readiness, app screenshots, or development links. Do not add a false claim that development has not begun.

**Why:** The user explicitly designated this as an initial idea submission. The participant guide separately permits prior projects while requiring documentation of the starting version and rights; presentation framing cannot waive disclosure requirements.

**How to apply:** Use prospective language for capabilities, architecture, testing, and deliverables. Prioritize admission criteria over final judging criteria. Treat survey results as exploratory evidence of needs and concept acceptance, never evidence of product performance.
