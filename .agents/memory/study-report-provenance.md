---
name: Study report provenance
description: Boundaries for reporting practice strengths and daily streaks from saved data.
---

Keep reading progress, automatic practice estimates, and formally passed assessments separate. A partial recitation must not become a mastered hadith just because its few attempted words matched.

**Why:** The user asked for a study-library experience with a contextual assistant and an overview of strong passages and streaks. Existing saved practice snapshots are consented evidence, not certified grades or a complete study-activity ledger.

**How to apply:** Require whole-hadith coverage before describing practice as strong. For current streaks, disclose that dates are report-save dates in the browser's local calendar, not all actual study days; imported or deleted reports cannot establish an immutable activity history. A future real study-day streak needs a separately defined activity event and timezone policy.
